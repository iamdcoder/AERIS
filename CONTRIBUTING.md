# Contributing

## Branches

- `main` — integration branch
- `engine-dev` — Person 1
- `copilot-product-dev` — Person 2

## Ownership

Person 1 owns `engine/`, `data/`, and `scenarios/`.
Person 2 owns `copilot/`, `api/`, and `frontend/`.
`contracts/` changes are coordinated.

Do not casually change contract field names or response shapes.
